CromX Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 02 August 2026

Welcome to CromX! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website https://www.cromx.shop and place orders as a guest.

✅ Guest Checkout: We respect your privacy. You can place orders without creating an account. We only collect the minimum information needed to process and deliver your order.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to process your orders and improve our services:

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email address, delivery address (house no., society, landmark, city, state, pincode).
  • Order Information: Product details, quantity, price, total amount, order date, payment method (COD).
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and time spent on our website.
  • Cookies: We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience and analyze website traffic.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Order Processing: To confirm your order, process payment (COD), and arrange delivery.
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries and provide assistance.
  • Order Updates: To send you order confirmations, delivery updates, and status notifications via SMS/WhatsApp/Email.
  • Service Improvement: To analyze trends, monitor website performance, and improve our products and services.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes.

3. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. However, we may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who help us operate our website, process orders, and deliver products (e.g., shipping partners, Firebase).
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law, we may disclose information to comply with legal obligations or protect our rights.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We use Firebase to securely store your order data. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

5. Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience on our website. Cookies are small data files stored on your device. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function properly.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent for data processing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@cromx.shop.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child, we will take steps to delete it.
